Trailer — 38 s Abysses: Meridian

The deeper you go, the darker it gets.

Abysses: Meridian is a first-person underwater exploration game: at the controls of a small submarine, and out in the water in a diving suit. Sunlight fades by forty metres. Below that, there is only what your searchlights touch, and what your sonar hears.

Abysses: Meridian favours immersion over simulation: easy to pilot, fun to fly, heavy with atmosphere. No hunger meters, no survival chores: just you, the dark, and the pressure.

Sink Your drop pod splashes down on Meridian and goes under, leaking. Patch it up, swim out with sixty seconds of air, and find the station that stopped answering three years ago.
Wake the station Thétis lies forty metres down. Get inside, bring its power back, make it your base, and find out what happened to its twelve crew.
Build your way down The cargo pods dropped with you sank deeper still. Every pod you recover and every material you salvage becomes an upgrade at the fabricator: thrusters, lights, sonar, drill, air, depth. Your pod gives way to a real submarine, and the dark gets a little further away.
Descend From sunlit kelp forests to drowned plains, sheer rifts and flooded caves hundreds of metres down, every layer of the ocean is darker, stranger and more hostile than the last. Your hull creaks under the pressure. Depth is the frontier.
See by searchlight Volumetric light, caustics, drifting particles and glowing life make the water feel thick and real. Switch your lights off and you vanish into the black, but you also go blind.
Listen A sonar ping reveals everything around you. Deep in the caves, something listens back.
Find the ring Something enormous lies far below, and since it woke, the sea has never stopped rising. It was not built by anyone you know. And it is calling.
