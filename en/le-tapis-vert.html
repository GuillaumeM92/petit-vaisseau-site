Twenty-four classic games, one green table.

A game of Belote with the family, a quiet Solitaire, Four in a Row between two coffees. Le Tapis Vert gathers the great classics of cards, boards and puzzles, with their real rules and opponents that really play.

Every finished game earns chips, even a lost one, and chips unlock new games. Those who prefer can open everything at once with a single, entirely optional purchase.

Cards · 10 games Belote, Solitaire, FreeCell, Crazy Eights, Up & Down the River, Whist, Hearts, Rummy, President and Golf.
Board & strategy · 7 games Four in a Row, Checkers, Reversi, Backgammon, Horse Race, Sea Battle and Radar.
Dice & puzzles · 7 games Yatzy, Sudoku, Word Guess, Quiz, Riddles, Pairs and 2048.
Your way Solo against the computer, at three levels. Several players on the same screen, everyone’s cards staying hidden between turns. Online with a friend using a game code (Up & Down the River and Crazy Eights).
No hassle The rules of every game always at hand, long games that pick up right where you left off, English or French as you like.

FAQ

Is the game really free?

On phones and tablets, yes. Several games are open from the start, the others unlock with the chips you win by playing. The "Unlock everything" purchase saves time, nothing more. There are no ads, no subscription and no chip packs for sale. On Steam, you buy the game once and everything is open from the start.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress stays on your device (and in Steam Cloud for the PC and Mac version). The details are in the privacy policy.

Can several people play?

Yes: up to four on the same screen, passing the phone around, or online with a friend for Up & Down the River and Crazy Eights. Empty seats are taken by the computer.

An iPhone or PC version?

Yes. The iPhone and iPad version is on the App Store, free like on Google Play. The PC and Mac version has been on Steam since October 14, 2026: paid, with every game open from the start, Steam achievements and Steam Cloud saves.

An idea, a bug, a house rule that differs?

Write to me at contact@petitvaisseau.com: I read everything and answer myself.
