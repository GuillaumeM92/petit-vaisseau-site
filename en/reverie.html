Rêverie: Audio Stories

Free · out now on PC, Mac and Android · coming soon to iPhone and iPad

Put on your headphones, or turn up the sound: the story begins. Rêverie brings you illustrated audio stories where you decide what happens next. A voice tells the tale, the characters answer, the rain and the wind close in around you — and at every turning point, you choose. Two complete stories are free, each with several endings.

Play free on Steam Google Play App Store — coming soon FAQ

Listen. Choose.

Rêverie is read with your ears. A narrator and the characters' voices carry the story, and around them, a whole world of sound: the storm over the ramparts, the hammer at the forge, the songs of a fairy tale. On screen, every place has its own illustration, gently brought to life by the glow of candles and lanterns.

When the story hands control to you, two large cards appear, left and right, and are read aloud. Show the guard the king’s seal, or say you are a mere merchant? Your decisions change what happens next, and how it ends.

Two complete stories are free from day one. More will join the library regularly.

You choose At every turning point, two cards. Your choices open different paths and lead to different endings: seven in the first story, five in the second.
An illustrated audio book Every place has its illustration, every chapter its title. Subtitles are optional, to follow the text or to listen in another language.
Replay for another ending Fast-forward skips the lines you have already heard: you are quickly back at the choice you want to make again.
Headphones or speakers Alone on headphones, or with the family around a phone, a tablet or a computer. Touch controls on mobile; keyboard, mouse or gamepad on computers.
Eight languages Voices in English and French. Subtitles in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian and Chinese.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. The game is free on Steam, the App Store and Google Play, with two complete stories: The Messenger of Corbeval and The King Who Could Not Sleep. There are no ads.

How do you play?

You listen. When a choice comes, two cards appear left and right, and are read aloud: tap the one you want on a phone or tablet, or click, use the keyboard or a gamepad on a computer. In some places, you also choose where to go next.

Do I need headphones?

No. On headphones or through the phone or computer speakers, as you like. Headphones bring the storm closer; speakers let several people listen together.

What age is it for?

The King Who Could Not Sleep is a gentle fairy tale for young and old, to listen to together. The Messenger of Corbeval is tenser: a stormy night, secrets and threats, better suited to older listeners.

Does the game collect any data?

No. No ads, no analytics, no personal data: your progress stays on your device. The details are in the privacy policy.

On which platforms?

On Windows and Mac through Steam, and on Android through Google Play. The iPhone and iPad version (App Store) is coming soon.
