Game studio · France

A studio of one, and one ship at a time.

Petit Vaisseau is Guillaume Merle’s studio. Space Defender: Frontier Squadron hit 1.0 on Steam on September 25, 2026. Le Tapis Vert, 24 card and board games, is out on Steam, Android, iPhone and iPad. Rêverie, illustrated audio stories where you choose what happens next, is out free on Steam and Android, and coming soon to iPhone and iPad. Two more games are in the works on an in-house engine, both coming soon to Steam: Abysses: Meridian, an underwater exploration game, and Waymaker, a space exploration game.

See the games Press contact

The studio in three lines

One person

Guillaume Merle, a French developer, writes the code, the design and the text in the evenings and at weekends, alongside a full-time job.

A first game

Space Defender launched in Early Access in May 2025. Sixteen months of player feedback later, it reaches 1.0 with its campaign and its wingmen.

A way of working

Ship small, listen, fix. The wingmen system, now at the heart of the game, started as a player’s suggestion on Discord.
