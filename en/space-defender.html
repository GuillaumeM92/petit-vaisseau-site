1.0 trailer — 30 s Space Defender: Frontier Squadron

The frontier is falling. Hold the line.

The Drones have swept through three galaxies, relay by relay. Station after station goes dark. Command has one answer left: you, a ship, and whoever you can convince to fly with you.

Space Defender blends twin-stick space combat with RPG progression. Between fights you dock at stations to trade, buy ships, research upgrades and recruit pilots. In the field you take on thirteen kinds of contracts, from convoy escorts to holdouts, against Drones that each fight in their own way.

The campaign runs across three acts and thirteen story missions, alongside Admiral Voss, Ilse Marin and ARIA, from the first defence line at Aquarii to the final battle against the Nexus.

Squadron Recruit pilots at stations, hand them a ship from your hangar, fly with up to three of them. A downed wingman ejects: recover the pod before it is lost.
13 contract types Clash, escort, convoy, siege, elites, armada, swarm, fleet, holdout, hunt, rescue, salvage, strike — with modifiers: asteroid fields, elite leaders, reinforcements, a ticking clock.
Distinct enemies Snipers keep their distance, minelayers leave traps, carriers launch waves, jammers scramble your missiles, support hulls recharge their allies' shields.
Build your ship 13 ships, from the nimble Falcon to heavy destroyers, and over 60 items. Every part you fit changes how the ship flies and fights: energy is a budget.
Progression Ranks from Recruit to Admiral, a four-branch research tree, a mission journal, three difficulty modes.

FAQ

Do I need to have played Early Access?

No. The 1.0 is the complete game: the three-act campaign, wingmen, nine new contract types, new weapon families and a full balancing pass. Early Access owners get the update for free.

Is there controller support?

Yes, from the menus to combat, with full navigation. Mouse and keyboard remain the reference for aiming.

Was AI used to make the game?

Yes, in part, and it is worth saying plainly. I worked with an AI assistant on part of the code and on proofreading the English and French text.

The game design, the balancing, the writing and the creative decisions are mine: I decide what goes into the game and what comes out. The music is composed by Alkakrab, a human artist, used under licence (Sci-Fi Music Packs), and the sound effects come from ZapSplat.

Mac, Linux or mobile version?

The game is on Windows and macOS through Steam: one purchase gets you both. A mobile version, “Space Defender: Squadron”, is out on Google Play and the App Store, free to try. Nothing is promised for Linux today.

How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?

On Discord, ideally: I read everything there. That is where the wingmen system came from.
